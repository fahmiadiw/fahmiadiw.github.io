Where Mothers Drop Out: An Antenatal Care Cascade Diagnostic for Lampung

15 regencies and cities · 160,016 pregnant women · Dinas Kesehatan Provinsi Lampung routine data, 2022 (latest district-level release)

⚑ Why some numbers can't be taken at face value (full note)
  • Pesawaran reports 8,112 for facility births, first postnatal visit, complete postnatal care and vitamin A alike; Tulang Bawang Barat and Metro do the same with 4,801 and 2,648.
  • Bandar Lampung reports K6 exactly equal to K4 (19,631), above 100% of its target, so its #1 rank on K6 is unverified.
  • K1 equals K4 in Tulang Bawang Barat and Metro: no woman stopped after her first visit.
The drop-out is concentrated.

Five districts (Lampung Barat, Pringsewu, Way Kanan, Pesawaran, Lampung Tengah) reach K6 for fewer than 35% of pregnant women. They have 34% of pregnancies but 56% of the women lost between visit 4 and 6; Lampung Tengah alone accounts for 13,960.

The indicator you pick changes the ranking.

Lampung Barat ranks #7 on four visits and last on six, at 22%. Lampung Utara moves the other way, from #11 to #2 (84%), and Mesuji from #12 to #5. A dashboard still showing K4 would point support at the wrong districts.

The postnatal numbers look perfect because many were not counted separately.

In 7 of 15 districts, at least three different delivery and postnatal services share the exact same count; these districts hold 44% of the province's births. The provincial 91.7% complete-postnatal-care figure is likely overstated, and a routine consistency check would catch it at entry. Read the full data-quality note →

Method. Antenatal coverage uses estimated pregnant women as the denominator; delivery and postnatal indicators use estimated births. Flags: ≥3 of 4 delivery/postnatal counts identical, K1 = K4, K4 = K6, or any count above 100% of target.
Limits. 2022 is the latest district-level public release. Estimated denominators can push coverage above 100% where the real number of pregnancies is higher than projected.
