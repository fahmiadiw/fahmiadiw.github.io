Staff follow the cities.

Bandar Lampung and Metro hold 38.5% of the province's nurses for 14.7% of its people. 8 of 13 regencies, home to 65% of residents, sit below the provincial average of 270 frontline workers per 100k; Lampung Timur has 151.

The obvious explanation doesn't hold.

Across all 15 districts, TB detection rises with staffing (r = 0.80). Remove the two cities and it disappears (r = 0.17 across 13 regencies). Lampung Barat has 309 workers per 100k and the lowest detection (53%); Lampung Selatan detects 73% with 195. More staff alone is unlikely to close the TB gap.

Combining signals reorders the list.

On TB detection alone, Lampung Barat ranks first. Adding dengue and staffing puts Lampung Tengah and Lampung Utara on top, lifts Tulang Bawang Barat (#6 → #3) and Pesawaran (#11 → #6), and drops well-staffed Pesisir Barat (#5 → #12). The top four hold 3.7 million people, 39% of the province.
